Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Student Face Attendance mobile application (the “App”) collects, uses, stores and protects information. The App is used by schools, colleges and universities to record student attendance, optionally using on-device face detection. This policy applies to all users: administrators, teachers and students.

3. Camera use and on-device processing

The camera permission is requested only when you start a face scan or enrollment, never automatically at app start. Before the camera opens, we show a disclosure screen and you must actively agree.

  • Face detection runs entirely on your device. The camera frames are processed locally by Google’s on-device face detection model and are not uploaded.
  • Only the resulting photograph and the derived face template are sent to our servers, and only if face attendance is switched on.

4. Face attendance is optional

Face-based attendance is optional. Attendance can always be marked manually as present, absent or leave without any face scan. If you do not use the face feature, no photograph or face template is created or used for you.

5. How we use information

  • To provide attendance recording, reports and class statistics.
  • To let teachers and administrators of your institution view the records they manage.
  • To secure your account and prevent unauthorized access.

We do not sell your data, do not show advertisements, and do not use your data for profiling or behavioural targeting.

7. Access control and security

  • Database rows are protected by row-level security (RLS): users can only read rows belonging to their own institution, and role permissions further limit what teachers and students can see.
  • Photographs and face templates are stored in private cloud storage and are retrieved only through authenticated, authorized requests — they are not published, listed or linked publicly anywhere in the App or on this page.
  • Authentication and access are enforced server-side; they cannot be bypassed by the app alone.

8. Encryption

  • In transit: all communication between the App and our servers uses TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS).
  • At rest: data is encrypted at rest by our cloud provider, with encrypted backups maintained.
  • On device: face detection runs locally; nothing is processed on third-party servers for detection.

9. Retention

Records are kept for as long as your institution keeps them; we apply no automatic deletion schedule. When a student is deleted, their photograph, face template and attendance records are removed. Account data is deleted as described in section 10.

10. Your rights and account deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time.

  • In the App: open Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your login, name and email address from the App. Classes, students and attendance records remain with your institution.
  • If you cannot access the App: email smartfaceattendanceofficial@gmail.com from your registered address. We will complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • Students or parents: you may also ask your institution to delete a student’s data, or contact us directly.

11. Children and students

The App is intended for institutional use. Student accounts are created and managed by the institution on the student’s behalf, not by children directly. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from children. If you believe a child has provided us data directly, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Advertising and analytics

The App contains no advertising, no ad SDKs and no third-party analytics or tracking. No advertising identifier is used or collected.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows when it last changed. Material changes will be communicated in the App. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

For questions, concerns, or to exercise any right described above, contact us at smartfaceattendanceofficial@gmail.com.
