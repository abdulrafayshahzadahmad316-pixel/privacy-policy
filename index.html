This policy applies to all users: administrators, teachers and students.

3 Camera use and on-device processing

The camera permission is requested only when you start a face scan or enrollment, never automatically at app start. Before the camera opens, we show a disclosure screen and you must actively agree.

  • Face detection runs entirely on your device. Camera frames are processed locally by Google’s on-device face detection model and are not uploaded.
  • Only the resulting photograph and the derived face template are sent to our servers, and only if face attendance is switched on.

4 Face attendance — optional, adults 18+ only

Age restriction: 18+ Face-based attendance is optional and is only used for students aged 18 or older. Before any face photo is captured, the teacher or administrator must confirm in the app that the student is 18+.

Attendance for younger students is always recorded manually (present, absent or leave) without any face scan. In that case no photograph or face template is created or used. Face scan is never required to use the App.

5 How we use information

  • To provide attendance recording, reports and class statistics.
  • To let teachers and administrators of your institution view the records they manage.
  • To secure your account and prevent unauthorized access.
What we never do: We do not sell your data, do not show advertisements, and do not use your data for profiling or behavioural targeting.

7 Access control and security

  • Database rows are protected by row-level security (RLS): users can only read rows belonging to their own institution, and role permissions further limit what teachers and students can see.
  • Photographs and face templates are stored in private cloud storage and are retrieved only through authenticated, authorized requests — they are not published, listed or linked publicly anywhere.
  • Authentication and access are enforced server-side; they cannot be bypassed by the app alone.

8 Encryption

  • In transit: all communication between the App and our servers uses TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS).
  • At rest: data is encrypted at rest by our cloud provider, with encrypted backups maintained.
  • On device: face detection runs locally; nothing is processed on third-party servers for detection.

9 Retention

Records are kept for as long as your institution keeps them; we apply no automatic deletion schedule. When a student is deleted, their photograph, face template and attendance records are removed. Account data is deleted as described in section 10.

10 Your rights and account deletion

Delete Account
  • In the App: open Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your login, name and email address. Classes, students and attendance records remain with your institution.
  • If you cannot access the App: use the account deletion request page above, or email smartfaceattendanceofficial@gmail.com. We complete deletions within 30 days.
  • Students or parents: you may also ask your institution to delete a student’s data, or contact us directly.

11 Students and age requirements

The App is intended for institutional use (schools, colleges and universities). Student accounts are created and managed by the institution on the student’s behalf. Face scan and face photo capture are only used for students aged 18 or older; for younger students, attendance is recorded manually without any face scan. We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data directly, contact us and we will delete it.

12 Advertising and analytics

Clean & ad-free: The App contains no advertising, no ad SDKs, and no third-party analytics or tracking. No advertising identifier is used or collected.
