1. About Us

Student Face Attendance is a face-recognition based attendance management application developed for schools, colleges and universities. It helps institutions record student attendance quickly and accurately, with optional on-device face detection.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have regarding your data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

3. Facial Recognition Data

Because our App uses face recognition for attendance, this section provides important details about how facial data is handled.

What we collect

  • Face photographs — captured only during student enrollment or attendance scan, and only when face attendance is enabled.
  • Face templates — a numeric signature (mathematical representation) derived from your face photograph. The template is used for matching; it is not a copy of your photo.

Purpose

  • Student identification — to identify enrolled students during attendance scanning.
  • Attendance marking — to automatically mark present / absent based on face match.
  • Preventing proxy attendance — to ensure the correct student is marked present.
We do NOT use facial data for: Advertising, marketing, selling to third parties, profiling, or any purpose other than attendance verification as described above.

Age restriction — 18+ only

Face attendance is only used for students aged 18 or older. Before any face photo is captured, the teacher or administrator must confirm in the App that the student is 18 years of age or older. For younger students, attendance is always recorded manually without any face scan. No photograph or face template is created for minors.

On-device processing

  • Face detection runs entirely on your device. Camera frames are processed locally by Google's on-device face detection model (ML Kit) and are not uploaded to any server.
  • Only the resulting photograph and derived face template are sent to our servers, and only if face attendance is switched on.
  • The camera permission is requested only when you start a face scan or enrollment, never automatically at app start.

4. How We Use Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide attendance recording, reports and class statistics.
  • To let teachers and administrators of your institution view and manage the records they are responsible for.
  • To secure your account and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To fix bugs and improve the App's stability.
What we never do: We do not sell your data, do not show advertisements, and do not use your data for profiling or behavioral targeting.

5. Data Storage & Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

Encryption

  • In transit: All communication between the App and our servers uses TLS 1.2+ (HTTPS).
  • At rest: Data is encrypted at rest by our cloud provider (Supabase), with encrypted backups maintained.
  • On device: Face detection runs locally; nothing is processed on third-party servers for detection.

Access control

  • Database rows are protected by row-level security (RLS): users can only read rows belonging to their own institution.
  • Role-based permissions further limit what teachers and students can see.
  • Photographs and face templates are stored in private cloud storage and retrieved only through authenticated, authorized requests.
  • Authentication and access are enforced server-side and cannot be bypassed by the app alone.

7. Data Retention

  • Institutional records: Student data and attendance records are stored for as long as the school account remains active. We apply no automatic deletion schedule.
  • When a student is deleted: Their photograph, face template, and attendance records are removed from our systems.
  • Account data: Deleted as described in the Account Deletion section below.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for institutional use (schools, colleges, and universities). Student accounts are created and managed by the institution on the student's behalf.

  • Face scan and face photo capture are only used for students aged 18 or older.
  • For students under 18, attendance is recorded manually without any face scan. No photograph or face template is created.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data directly from minors.
  • If you believe a minor has provided us data directly, contact us and we will delete it promptly.
